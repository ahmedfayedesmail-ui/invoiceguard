# Vault UI timeout: diagnosis (Nov 28 2026, local, 1 CPU core, synthetic data, no source change)
Test: test/vault-ui.test.js "delete one import ... restore" (the one that failed once in CI run 46068796567 at the "Loaded files (2)" wait after Unlock, 30000 ms).
Method: an instrumented copy (vault-diag-raf-default.mjs, kept here, NOT in test/) logs the time of every step (LAP lines). Load = 3 busy loops on the 1-core sandbox (unloaded: whole test about 5 s).
Findings (all local):
1. Unloaded, every step is fast (restore about 0.8 s, unlock about 0.2 s).
2. Under load the same test fails mostly at the "Backup restored" wait (line 61 of the copy), not only at the unlock wait. Default polling: 7 failures in 10 runs (timeouts at 30 s / 15 s).
3. CAUSE (proven by a side-by-side check): the page showed "Backup restored and checked. Unlock with its password." about 1 s after the upload, and a Node-side poll saw it at that moment (run logs: message seen at ~12.4 s), while the in-page waitForFunction with Puppeteer's default polling ("raf", requestAnimationFrame) did not resolve until its timeout (~26 s) in 3 of 4 runs. The app is not slow; the test's wait stalls when the renderer is starved. The auto-lock (the test shrinks the 5 minute timer to 1.5 s) did not cause it: the gate stayed unlocked and the state after unlock reached "Loaded files (2)" in every run that got there.
4. Check of the fix: with polling: 100 on every waitForFunction, 9 of 10 runs passed under the same load; the 1 failure was a different symptom: a page error "TypeError: Cannot read properties of null (reading 'textContent')" caught by the final assert.deepEqual(errors, []). It did not repeat in 9 further runs, its source line was not captured (the stack logging was added after it), so it is OPEN and unexplained. It could be the app or the test's predicate racing a page change.
Not done: no test edited, no timeout raised. Proposed (not applied): polling: 100 on the waits in this file, plus capturing the stack of any page error, then 20+ loaded runs; the page error stays a separate open risk until it is reproduced with a stack.
