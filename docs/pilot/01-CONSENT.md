# Pilot consent and authorization (one seller, one period)

This is a controlled pilot of InvoiceGuard's "problem loop", a Concierge Pilot. It is not an automation and not a compliance or tax service. Nothing here promises that Amazon will accept anything.

## What the seller agrees to, in plain words
1. You choose to take part. You can stop at any time and delete everything (step 6 of the procedure).
2. You run it on your own computer, in your own browser, on the page `/local/loop`. Your files never leave your machine. We do not ask for, receive, store or look at your documents, your Amazon data, your password or your recovery key.
3. You, not us, hold the vault password and the recovery key. If both are lost, the data cannot be recovered by anyone. We cannot help recover it.
4. You decide what to share with us afterwards. The only thing we ask for is the **aggregate results file** (numbers only). You read it before you send it. You may also tell us in your own words what was useful or wrong.
5. You confirm you are authorized to use the Amazon account and the accounting data you load, and that the documents contain no data you are not allowed to process on your own computer.
6. You understand that the tool reads two files you give it and reports differences. It does not change your accounting system, does not write to Amazon, and does not upload anything to Amazon.
7. The tool's findings are leads to investigate, not conclusions about your legal or tax position. Things you declare (for example "this export is complete") are your declarations and are shown as such. They are not verified.

## Not part of this pilot
Public uploads, any hosted processing, AI services, analytics, cloud backups made by us, and the older `/local` page (do not use it for seller documents).

## Record
Date of consent, the seller's reference name chosen by the seller (not necessarily a company name), and the period are written by the seller in their own notes. We keep no copy.
